Privacy Policy for Word Puzzle

Effective Date: November 2, 2026 | Last Updated: November 2, 2026

Quick Summary: Word Puzzle is a client-side word-search game. We respect your privacy. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal information such as your name, email, or address. All gameplay progress (levels, coins, scores) is stored locally on your device. Third-party advertising partners (Google AdMob) may collect limited device identifiers to deliver ads in compliance with Google Play Developer policies.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information You Provide:

We do not require you to create an account, log in, or provide any personally identifiable information (PII) to play Word Puzzle.

B. Automatically Collected Information (Gameplay Data):

Your game progress, completed levels, unlocked categories, coin balance, and audio settings are saved strictly on your local device storage using Android SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers.

C. Information Collected by Third Parties (Google AdMob):

We use Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded video ads. Google AdMob may collect and process information such as:

  • Device information (such as model, operating system version, screen resolution)
  • Google Advertising ID (AAID)
  • IP address (for coarse geographic location to serve relevant ads)
  • Ad interaction data (such as views, clicks, and rewarded ad completion)

For more details on how Google processes ad data, please visit Google Advertising Privacy & Terms.

2. How We Use Information

Any data processed is used exclusively for:

  • Providing and saving your local gameplay progress, coin balance, and puzzle states.
  • Displaying interstitial ads and rewarded ads to unlock extra hints and bonus coins.
  • Ensuring app stability, performance, and fraud prevention in advertising.

3. Device Permissions Used

Word Puzzle requests only the minimal permissions required for normal operation:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to fetch and display ads.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used by the Google Mobile Ads SDK to check network availability.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used exclusively to provide tactile haptic feedback during word swiping (can be toggled on/off in the app).

We do not access sensitive permissions such as location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage files.

4. Third-Party Services

Word Puzzle integrates third-party services that have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Our app does not knowingly collect any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). If you believe a child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the app, please contact us immediately, and we will take steps to ensure appropriate safeguards or deletion.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all gameplay data (coins, levels, high scores) is stored locally on your device, you have complete control over it. You can erase all data at any time by clearing the app data in Android Settings or by uninstalling the application.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA/CPRA)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including:

  • The right to opt out of personalized advertising via your device settings: Settings > Google > Ads > Delete or Reset Advertising ID.
  • The right to access or delete any personal data held by advertising partners.

8. Security

We take the security of your information seriously. Because no personal data is transferred to external custom servers, the risk of data compromise is minimal. All ad transmissions are conducted over industry-standard encrypted HTTPS connections.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage users to periodically review this page for any updates.
